# TolongApp — design direction

## Theme Name
Serene Signal

## Intro
A calm, mobile-first emergency companion that makes preparedness feel approachable through flat-vector characters, gentle natural color, and clear next steps.

## Design movement
Friendly flat vector illustration inspired by modern language-learning apps: rounded geometry, clean outlines, expressive eyes, and no gradients.

## Core principles
- Calm before clever: surface the next safe action first.
- Context changes character posture and expression.
- Legibility under stress: generous spacing, large touch targets, short copy.
- Offline-first feel: show local state and cached data as trustworthy.

## Color philosophy
Sage green, muted sky blue, warm amber/terracotta, and earthy neutrals on a warm ivory canvas.

## Layout paradigm
Single-column mobile dashboard with a compact header, one primary status card, and stacked cards that can be scanned quickly.

## Signature elements
- Context-aware character illustration card.
- Soft rounded cards with thin borders instead of heavy shadows.
- Pill-shaped status labels and a persistent bottom navigation.

## Interaction philosophy
Tap to reveal detail, use progressive disclosure, and make every alert actionable without panic.

## Animation
Subtle float on the character and gentle pressed states only; no distracting motion.

## Typography system
Use a warm system sans stack with compact uppercase labels, high-contrast headings, and comfortable body copy.

## Brand essence and voice
A prepared neighbor who says: "You’re not alone. Here’s what to do next."

## Wordmark/logo
TolongApp wordmark paired with a simple four-petal signal mark.

## Signature brand color
Sage green #63896d.
